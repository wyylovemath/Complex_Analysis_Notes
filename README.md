---

## 📖 Overview & Table of Contents

The notes are structured into two primary chapters:

### Chapter 1: Cauchy's Theorem
* **1.1 Goursat's Theorem** — Intuition, detailed proof via triangle bisections, and corollaries for rectangular contours.
* **1.2 Cauchy's Theorem in Discs** — Local existence of primitives and integration over disc boundaries/toy contours.
* **1.3 Cauchy's Integral Formula (CIF)** — Keyhole contour proof, Cauchy Inequalities, Power Series Expansion Theorem, Liouville's Theorem, and the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra.
* **1.4 Identity Theorem** — Uniqueness of analytic functions and introduction to Analytic Continuation.
* **1.5 Applications & Core Theorems**
  * Morera's Theorem (converse of Cauchy's Theorem)
  * Sequences of Holomorphic Functions (Holomorphic Limit Theorem & Uniform Convergence of Derivatives)
  * Holomorphic Functions defined via Integrals
  * Schwarz Reflection Principle & Symmetry Principle
  * Runge's Approximation Theorem (Rational approximation & Pole Pushing Lemma)
* **1.6 Generalizations of Cauchy's Theorem** — Homotopy Invariance, primitive existence in simply connected domains, and topological implications.

### Chapter 2: Singularities & Meromorphic Functions
* **2.1 Zeros and Poles** — Local factorization, orders of zeros/poles, principal part expansions, residues, and residue computation formulas.
* **2.2 Cauchy's Residue Theorem** — Proof using keyhole contours and generalizations to multiple poles/toy contours.
* **2.3 Classifications of Singularities**
  * Removable Singularities (Riemann's Removable Singularity Theorem)
  * Essential Singularities (Casorati-Weierstrass Theorem)
* **2.4 Meromorphic Functions** — Behavior in the extended complex plane ($\hat{\mathbb{C}}$) and characterization of rational functions.
* **2.5 Argument Principle & Applications**
  * Argument Principle
  * Rouché's Theorem
  * Open Mapping Theorem
  * Maximum Modulus Principle
